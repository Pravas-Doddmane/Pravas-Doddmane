## 🚀 Projects

### 🌟 SessionStars: Session Conducting & Attendance Platform

A production-ready full-stack platform for conducting sessions, managing participants, and recording secure QR-based attendance with geofencing validation. The platform supports participant invitations, Google OAuth2 authentication, feedback collection, resources, notices, analytics, and cloud deployment.

* 🔹 Secure Google OAuth2 authentication with JWT authorization  
* 🔹 Invite-link based participant onboarding with administrator approval  
* 🔹 Dynamic QR attendance with configurable expiration and GPS geofencing  
* 🔹 Attendance analytics, Excel report export, and participant management  
* 🔹 Resources, FAQs, notices, feedback collection, and responsive dashboards  
* 🔹 Fully deployed with Netlify (Frontend), Render (Backend), and Neon PostgreSQL  

#### 🛠 Tech Stack
`Java 17` | `Spring Boot` | `Spring Security` | `JWT` | `Google OAuth2` | `Spring Data JPA` | `Hibernate` | `PostgreSQL (Neon)` | `React` | `Tailwind CSS` | `React Query` | `Axios` | `Maven`

🔗 [Live Demo](https://sessionstars.netlify.app/) • 💻 [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Pravas-Doddmane/SessionStar_Session_Conducting_Platform)
